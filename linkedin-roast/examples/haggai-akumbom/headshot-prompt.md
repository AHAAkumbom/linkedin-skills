# Headshot prompt

Paste this into the image tool you choose, and **attach your own photo**.

> Using the attached photo of me, create a clean, professional shoulders-up headshot. Keep my real face, features, skin tone and hairstyle exactly as they are: do not slim, smooth, reshape or change my appearance. Use a plain, neutral light-grey background and soft, even, front-facing light with no harsh shadows. Dress me in a clean, simple outfit that suits a health researcher and dentist (for example a plain shirt or blazer, or a clean white clinical coat). Look straight at the camera with a relaxed, natural expression. Square crop, sharp focus on the face, no text, no logos, no filters.

## Three notes
1. LinkedIn says your photo must reflect your likeness. If the tool changes your face, don't use the result.
2. Use a square crop of at least 400 × 400 pixels, JPG or PNG, under 8 MB.
3. Your photo goes to whichever image tool you use, so read its privacy terms first.
